About

TinyFun is a minimalist site that collects some of my favorite PICO-8 games and makes them super convenient to play and sift through.

The PICO-8 player is separated from each game and cached, so you only download ~20-50KB per game. The games also get hotswapped into the same player, so you don't have to reload the page like other webgame sites.

All games included on this site are Creative Commons (usually CC4-BY-NC-SA) or have been added with explicit permission by the creator.

If you want to add a game, have any questions, or just want to say hi, please feel free to Email me!
