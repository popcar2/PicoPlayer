About

PicoPlayer is a non-profit, open source, minimalist site that collects some of my favorite PICO-8 games and makes them super convenient to play and sift through. No ads, no shovelware, no gotchas.

The website is partially inspired by UFO 50, a collection of great retro-style games that follows the same concept: letting you explore a bunch of quality games without getting in your way.

All games included on this site are Creative Commons (usually CC4-BY-NC-SA) or have been added with explicit permission by the creator.

PicoPlayer was made by popcar2 (admittedly, with help from Claude).

If you want to add a game, have any questions, or just want to say hi, please feel free to Email me!
