Crimson Night

How long will you last? How many demons can you banish back to hell?

This is my very first attempt at making a game.

It’s been a challenging but incredibly rewarding ride—lots of trial and error, learning as I go, and plenty of moments where I got stuck. But I’ve finally managed to bring it to a playable state.

I used AI-assisted coding mainly for repetitive tasks, debugging, and a few small things where I was stuck and couldn’t find the right solution on my own. But zero AI was used for the visuals, sprites, graphics, animations, SFX, or music. Those are all made by me.

Still a long way to go, but getting it this far feels pretty damn good.


🎮 v.1.5 — The Crimson Ascension

You asked. I listened. The darkness has been improved.

🦇 Level Up Banner: No more accidental power-ups. Choose your fate like a proper supernatural hunter.

💀 Damage Feedback: Getting hit now actually looks painful. You’re welcome.

❤️ Blood for the Bloodthirsty: Every 50 kills, enemies now drop Hearts to restore your HP. You should still pick it up, but murder has never been so nutritious.

🏃‍♂️‍➡️ Dash of the Damned: Dash now doubles as a dodge. Slip into the enemy line and become briefly untouchable. Because getting hit is for mortals.

🎵 New & Extended Music: More gothic atmosphere. More tunes to die to.

🏆 High Score Board: Challenge the Dark Lords and show them who really runs this night. Screenshots only, because apparently we're not made of money.

⚖️ Difficulty Rebalance: You might actually survive longer now. No promises, hunter.

✨ Quality of Life: Polished some visuals, squashed some bugs, and generally made the night slightly less crimson.
