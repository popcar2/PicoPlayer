Polder by SirVer

You inherit drowned land behind a sea dike. Raise dikes, dig canals and build windmill chains to pump it dry, basin by basin, like the mills of Kinderdijk. Your workers carry out every order and haul every load of soil and wood through your towns, so distance and town placement are part of the puzzle.

How It Works

Goal: get every non-sea cell dry. There are scenarios that make this harder.

Land height runs from -8 to +4, 0 is sea level. Water flows toward the lower surface.

Mills pump in their needle's direction and lift water by at most 2. Deep basins need chains of mills, and the last mill must pump over the dike into the sea. Black needle = pumping, gray = idle, red = lift too high.

Mills stand only on >= +1 dikes. Most tools place job flags; the nearest idle worker walks over and does the job.

Digging yields soil, raising spends it, and the sea can be dredged for endless soil. Trees give wood. Fields grow food on dry peat below sea level, and surplus food grows your workforce.

Floods drown fields, towns and the people in them.

Hint: At the beginning food is running out fast, find a shallow water basin (-1 or -2), raise a dike and pump water out. Then place fields there. Once food is secured, you can concentrate on the rest of basin.

Controls

D-pad - Move the cursor
[X] - Do what the cursor icon shows. Hold [X] and move to repeat raise, dig, farm, plant, fell or cancel
[O] - Tap to cancel the job flag under the cursor. Hold [O], pick a tool with the d-pad, release
[O] + [X] - Close a message

Modes

Tutorial - start here
Archipelago - reclaim everything
High Lake - water above and below, 30-minute deadline
The Bowl - at most four towns
Time Trial - a 5-digit seed picks the map
