POOM by freds72

Mars base, Union Aerospace Corporation experiments opened a portal to Hell. You appear to be the last human, fight your way out!

If experiencing slowdowns, prefer desktop browser or beefy mobile.

How to Play

Click on the game to enable mouse support (browser security).

Desktop mouse controls are recommended, and automatically selected if a mouse is detected. Mouse sensitivity can be changed from the controls menu.

Without a mouse, go to the controls menu on the title screen to switch between control schemes (scheme 2 is preferred).

Mobile menu controls: use the left paddle to navigate, and up/down on the right paddle to cancel/select.
