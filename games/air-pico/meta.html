Air Pico by Mot

A cozy flight sim lite for PICO-8.

Become a pilot for Pico Air, a small air company operating between the islands of Pico Isles.
Perform deliveries, charter flights, crop dusting and other jobs to unlock new planes.

Features:

  • A 16km x 16km map to explore
  • 3 different planes
  • External camera views
  • Photo mode

Controls

  • Arrows - Ailerons and elevator (roll and pitch)
  • O + Up/Down - Throttle
  • O + Left/Right - Rudder
  • X + Up/Down - Adjust elevator trim
  • X + Left/Right - Flaps

Or use player 2 controls for throttle and rudder.

Dashboard instruments from left to right are:

  • Speed in knots
  • Height in feet
  • Vertical speed in feet per minute
  • Flaps
  • Throttle

Photo mode

During a mission or free flight you can select "Photo mode" from the PICO-8 pause menu.
Photo mode allows pauses the game and allows you to move the camera independently from the plane.

During photo mode:

  • Arrows - Turns the camera
  • Player 2 arrows - Moves the camera
  • X (hold) - Move slowly
  • O (hold) - Move quickly
  • X+O (hold) - Move very quickly

Select "Back to game" from the pause menu to exit photo mode and return to the game.
