Pet the cat

Pet the cat is pico8's most accurate cat petting simulator
Get settled with a kittie that is all yours to enjoy.


features:

  • A wide variety of cute kitties
  • Very acccurate cat behaviour modelled after real life cats
  • A disembodied hand for you to pet the kittie
  • The disembodied hand now comes with mouse support
  • Real cat feel
  • Thanks to mouse support even more real cat feel (can be toggled in pause menu)
  • Nice looking background wallpaper
  • Ultra realistic cat sounds (now includes purring)
  • Lots of scritches
  • Allergen free
  • Even though it may want to, it cannot scratch or bite you (though it may hiss)
  • Unlike its real life counterpart, this kittie cannot escape you.
  • Did I mention the realistic cat behaviour?
  • Spicy kittie behaviour when annoyed
  • real purring
  • Hissing can be turned off in the menu
  • Mouse support can be turned off in menu for people who don't like mouse lock.

Some hints on getting to enjoy a nice purr:
Like real cats, petting against the grain is not recommended, but head scratches reliably are!
