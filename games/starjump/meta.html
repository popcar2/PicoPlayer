Starjump by lucatron

You are a star that jumps (video gaming).

Soundtrack: youtube.com/watch?v=qi2jSi0W4JM

Move: arrows or ESDF
Jump: Z/X/N/M

Addition options in menu (P/Enter):

  • Adjust wobble intensity
  • Show timer
  • Skip screen
  • Reset progress
