willthemangetfrog by MarechalBanane

Hey everyone!

Here is Will The Man Get Frog, a chill little game about composing silly or sweet haikus (short poems) and sharing them with the world!

I wanted to make a game about constrained creation that felt easy to play, in the hope that people end up surprised by how fun it is to write stuff. It was my first serious project as an indie dev and it was published on Steam on February 14th 2024. Hope you'll like it!
If you do, consider following me on Bluesky or itch.io!
